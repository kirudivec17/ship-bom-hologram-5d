📜 SOLAS Ch. II-2 Regulation 15.2.4

Fire Control Plans must be permanently displayed for guidance of ship officers. One duplicate set of fire control plans shall be stored in a prominently marked weather-tight enclosure outside the deckhouse for shore side fire-fighting personnel.

🧯 Primary Extinguishers & Fire Lines

Main Fire Line with minimum 2 jets of water simultaneously. CO2 Fixed Fire-Extinguishing System for Engine Room with 2-stage release alarm and automatic fan shut-offs.

🚪 Structural Fire Protection (A/B/C Class)

A-Class Divisions: Steel bulkheads insulated to prevent temperature rise > 139°C for 60 mins (A-60).
B-Class: Non-combustible material (30 mins fire resistance).

🏷️ IMO Symbols Standard - Resolution A.952(23)

All graphical symbols used on fire control plans must comply with IMO Resolution A.952(23) and A.654(16). Standardised symbology ensures global fire brigade interoperability.
